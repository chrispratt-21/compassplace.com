The Problem

Marketplace leads are time-sensitive. Most agencies aren't built for that.

When a family submits an inquiry on A Place for Mom, Caring.com, or a similar platform, they hear from multiple agencies at the same time. The agency that reaches them first builds trust and sets expectations before anyone else gets a chance.

But responding immediately is hard. A lead arrives while your owner is meeting with a new client. Or your salesperson is on a caregiver intake call. Or it's 7pm on a Friday. The referral sits, and by the time someone gets to it, another agency has already had a full conversation with the family.

📅

You're already with someone

Your best salespeople are often in the field or on calls. A new lead arriving during that time has no one to greet it.

🌙

After-hours gaps

Families often submit inquiries in the evening or on weekends. Most agencies aren't staffed to respond until the next business day.

💸

Wasted lead spend

Agencies pay hundreds of dollars per referral lead, then lose the conversion because response time lags behind the competition.

How It Works

Simple setup. Immediate impact.

FCA connects to your existing marketplace lead flow in minutes and starts responding the same day.

1

Download the app

Get the FCA app for iOS or Android and create your agency profile. This is how you activate and release coverage, and how leads and call notes reach you.

Available on the App Store and Google Play. Setup takes a few minutes.
2

Forward your leads

Set up a forwarding rule so marketplace lead notifications come to our dedicated FCA email address. That's it for setup.

Works with A Place for Mom, Caring.com, and any marketplace that sends email notifications.
3

We make the call

Our on-duty advocate receives an instant alert and calls the family on your behalf, aiming to reach them before any other agency does. Advocates introduce your agency, answer initial questions, and build rapport.

Coverage hours are configured to match your schedule and business needs. On the rare occasion the same lead reaches more than one FCA agency, we follow a clear, consistent priority system to decide who we respond on behalf of, so every agency gets fair access to the coverage they've signed up for.
4

You take it from there

Qualified, interested families are handed off to your team. You get a complete record of the contact in your email, including notes from the call.

Full lead history, contact details, and call notes are available.
